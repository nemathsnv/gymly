2. What Gymly is

Gymly is a personal training log. It lets you:

  • plan routines;
  • log workouts, sets, repetitions, weights and effort;

  • see your progress across your devices.

It is currently free of charge. If we ever plan to charge for anything, we will tell you in advance, and you will never be charged without agreeing to it first.
