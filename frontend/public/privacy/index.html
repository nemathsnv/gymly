Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: Gymly is a workout and body-weight tracker. We store what you log so you can use it across your devices. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not use tracking or analytics. You can export your data at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account. AI features only run if you turn them on. An Oura integration is planned but not available yet; section 8 explains how it will work.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Gymly service at gymly.churntech.com. It also covers the Gymly mobile apps when they are connected to that service. In this policy, "Gymly", "we" and "us" mean the operator of that service.

Gymly is run by an individual based in the European Union. It is not run by a company. For this service, the operator is the controller of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: nemathsnv@gmail.com.

Gymly is open-source software based on openGym by Duarte Santos. Its source code is public at github.com/nemathsnv/gymly. Anyone can run their own copy. This policy covers only the copy at gymly.churntech.com. If you use a copy run by someone else, that person decides how your data is handled.

2. What we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. Most of it is information you enter yourself.

2.1 Account and sign-in

  • Profile: a random profile ID, the display name you choose (it does not need to be your real name), and the date you created the profile. If you used an invite code to sign up, we keep that code too.
  • Passkeys: Gymly signs you in with passkeys (WebAuthn). We store only the passkey's public key, a usage counter, when it was created and last used, and a device label such as "Chrome · Android". Your fingerprint, face scan or device PIN never leaves your device, and we never receive it.
  • Password and email (only where password sign-in is offered): the service can be set to allow sign-in with a name and password. Password sign-in is not currently offered on gymly.churntech.com. If it is turned on, we store your password only as a salted one-way hash (scrypt). You may also add an email address, but only as a sign-in name. We never send email to it, and we never use it for marketing.
  • Session: a signed session cookie keeps you signed in (see section 4). A phone paired with your account uses an equivalent signed token.

We do not ask for your real name, postal address, phone number or payment details.

2.2 Training and body data

This is the information you choose to log:

  • Workouts, exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, durations and rest times. This includes the effort you record per set (reps in reserve, RIR, or rating of perceived exertion, RPE), personal records, and notes on workouts and sets.
  • Routines, training plans, schedules, progression settings and your equipment (for example plates and dumbbells).
  • Body weight and goal weight, and body measurements. Measurements include neck, chest, waist, hips, limbs, body-fat percentage and any custom fields you create.
  • Habits and day notes, if you use them. Examples: alcohol-free days and drink counts, and days marked as sick or away.
  • Your sex and birth year, if you fill in "About you". These are used to work out strength levels.
  • Gym membership card codes, if you store them in the app.
  • App settings, such as language, units, theme, reminder time and time zone.

Some of this, such as body measurements, body-fat percentage, health-related notes or injuries you tell the AI Coach about, can count as data concerning health. The GDPR gives this kind of data extra protection. Gymly only stores it because you enter it.

2.3 Photos and videos

You can attach photos and videos to custom exercises and to workouts. Progress photos work the same way. These files are stored on our server under your profile. Before upload, your device re-encodes photos and strips metadata from videos. This removes location (GPS) data and other camera metadata.

2.4 Notifications

If you allow notifications for rest timers or reminders, we store your browser's push subscription. That is an address at your browser's push service plus the keys used to encrypt messages to it. We also store a random device identifier.

2.5 Technical and security data

  • Connection data: your IP address, the address of the page you request, and your browser's user-agent string. Our network provider and web servers process these to deliver pages and protect the service, and some of it appears in short-lived server logs (see section 9). We also use IP addresses, held only in memory, to limit repeated sign-in attempts.
  • Security log: we keep a log of account and admin events, such as sign-ins, passkeys added or removed, and profiles created or deleted. Each entry records the profile ID, the display name, the event and the time. Depending on the server's settings, it may also include a shortened or full IP address.
  • Live session status: while you train, the server briefly holds which workout you are doing and how far along you are, so the operator's dashboard can show who is training. This is held in memory for about a minute and is never written to disk.

2.6 Guest mode

If you choose "Continue without account", everything stays in your browser on that device and is never sent to our server. We cannot see it, back it up or recover it.

4. Cookies and local storage

Gymly sets one cookie, __Host-gymsid, and only after you sign in. It holds a signed session token that keeps you signed in. It cannot be read by scripts on the page (HttpOnly), is sent only over HTTPS (Secure), and is not sent with requests from other sites (SameSite=Lax). It expires at the end of the configured session lifetime, which is 90 days by default, or earlier when you sign out. The service cannot work without it, so it does not need a consent banner.

The app also stores data on your device so it works quickly and offline. It uses your browser's local storage, IndexedDB and a service-worker cache. The Android and iOS apps use files and the system's secure storage instead. These copies of your data stay on your device. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app removes them.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, tracking pixels, third-party scripts or third-party fonts.

5. Hosting and service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Gymly. They process data only so the service can run.

  • Hostinger International Ltd. provides the virtual private server that runs Gymly and stores your account and training data. The server is in the European Union (Germany).
  • Cloudflare, Inc. carries all traffic between your device and our server and provides the HTTPS connection. This means your requests, including the data you sync, pass through Cloudflare's network, and Cloudflare processes your IP address and connection data. Cloudflare runs a global network and is based in the United States (see section 12).
  • DigitalOcean, LLC stores our off-site backups in its Frankfurt (Germany) data centre. Backups are encrypted on our server before upload, with a key DigitalOcean does not have, so DigitalOcean cannot read them.
  • Push services deliver notifications. Which one depends on your browser: for example Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft. The service receives your push address and an encrypted message, such as "Rest is over" or the name of today's routine.
  • GitHub (Microsoft) hosts the Android app's releases. When the Android app checks for updates, it contacts GitHub, which sees your IP address.

The operator can access the data stored on the server to run and support the service. For example, the admin dashboard shows the list of profiles, body weight and workouts. We do not disclose your data to anyone else, except to the AI or integration providers you choose to connect (section 6, section 7), or where the law requires it.

6. AI features

Gymly has two optional AI features. Neither runs unless you turn it on.

6.1 AI Coach

If the AI Coach is offered on the service and you give your consent, Gymly sends a selected set of your data to an AI provider to get training feedback. The provider is chosen by the operator, for example Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. The consent screen lists exactly what is sent. The data is:

  • your plan;
  • your recent training, at most the last 12 weeks or 60 sessions;
  • your body weight and goal weight;
  • the answers you gave in the Coach's intake, including any injuries or limitations you mention;
  • your unit and language preferences;
  • if you opt in to community comparison, anonymous medians from other people who share.

You are identified to the provider only by a pseudonym, never by your name or profile ID. You can stop using the Coach at any time, and "Reset everything" deletes your Coach data on our server. In the mobile app, you can use your own API key instead. In that case your data goes directly from your phone to the provider you chose, under your own agreement with that provider.

6.2 AI assistant connections

If this feature is enabled for your profile, you can connect an AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude to your Gymly data. You connect it through an authorisation screen or with a read-only access token. Once connected, the assistant can read your routines, workouts, body weight and related statistics. Your habit log, sex and birth year are always withheld. Depending on the connection type, the assistant can also propose changes to your plan, which you apply and can undo. The assistant's provider processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can disconnect assistants and revoke tokens at any time in Settings.

Data imported from Oura is never sent to either of these features (see section 8).

7. Imports and other integrations

  • File imports (Strong, Hevy, FitNotes, Gravl, Apple Health export, CSV): the file is read on your device, and only the workouts or weigh-ins you import are saved to your profile.
  • Hevy API import: your Hevy API key is used directly from your browser to fetch your workouts from Hevy. We do not store it.
  • Health Connect (Android app): if you turn it on, Gymly writes your workouts to Health Connect on your phone. Gymly does not read other data from Health Connect.

8. Planned Oura integration

Status: not available yet. We plan to let you connect an Oura account to Gymly through the Oura API (version 2). We are registering Gymly as an application with Oura. Today Gymly cannot connect to Oura and receives no Oura data. Before the integration is launched, we will update this section if any detail below changes.

8.1 What it will do

Connecting Oura will be optional. If you connect, Gymly will import Oura data so you can see it next to your training, for example how you slept and recovered around your workouts. Only data types you authorise will be imported. We plan to request access to:

  • sleep (sleep periods and daily sleep summaries);
  • readiness (daily readiness summaries);
  • activity (daily activity summaries);
  • heart rate;
  • stress (daily stress summaries);
  • workouts recorded by Oura.

We will request only the Oura permissions these features need. We do not plan to request your Oura email address or personal information (age, sex, height, weight).

8.2 Permission and your Oura credentials

  • You connect on Oura's own authorisation page (OAuth 2.0). There you see which data types Gymly asks for and can allow or refuse each one. Gymly never sees your Oura password.
  • Oura gives Gymly an access token and a refresh token. These are kept only on Gymly's server. They are never shown in the app, sent to your browser, or shared with anyone. They are used only to fetch the data you authorised.
  • We will not ask for more Oura data than you authorised without asking you again first.

8.3 How Oura data will and will not be used

  • Oura data will be used only to show you your own information in Gymly. Like the rest of your data, the operator can access it on the server, but it will not be shown to other users.
  • Oura data will never be sent to an AI model or AI service. That includes Gymly's AI Coach, AI assistant connections such as ChatGPT or Claude, and any other model. It will never be used to train, fine-tune or evaluate AI.
  • Oura data will never be sold, rented or licensed. It will never be shared with or disclosed to third parties, except the hosting providers in section 5 that store and carry it for us. It will never be used for advertising, marketing or targeting. We will not use it to contact you.

8.4 Disconnecting and deleting

  • You will be able to disconnect Oura at any time in Gymly's Settings, and to revoke Gymly's access from your Oura account. Revoking takes effect immediately: Gymly stops fetching and processing your Oura data. When you disconnect in Gymly, we delete the tokens and ask Oura to revoke them.
  • When you ask us to delete your Oura data, or delete your Gymly account, we will delete all Oura data we imported for you within 72 hours, and we will not keep any of it afterwards. You can ask by disconnecting with the delete option or by emailing us.
  • While you stay connected, we will keep imported Oura data only as long as the features you use need it.

8.5 Oura is separate from Gymly

Oura processes your data under its own privacy policy. Oura and Gymly are separate, independent controllers. For data held by Oura, contact Oura. For copies imported into Gymly, contact us: Oura cannot delete data that has already been shared with us. Gymly is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Oura. "Oura" is a trademark of its owner.

9. How long we keep data

  • Account and training data: for as long as your profile exists, or until you delete it with "Reset everything" or ask us to delete your account.
  • Photos and videos: when you remove a photo or video, the file is deleted from the server 14 days later. When your account is deleted, all your files are deleted with it.
  • Security log: entries are deleted after 90 days, or sooner once the log reaches 5,000 events.
  • Server logs: logs that may contain IP addresses and request details have a fixed size limit and are overwritten as new entries arrive.
  • Sign-in links, pairing codes and passkey challenges: expire within minutes.
  • Off-site backups: encrypted backups of the server's data are taken every six hours. We keep the last four, one per day for 30 days and one per week for 12 weeks, then delete them. So data you delete can stay in an encrypted backup for up to about 12 weeks. Backups are used only to restore the service after a failure.
  • Pre-update snapshots: before each software update, a snapshot of the server's data is saved on the same server so a failed update can be undone. These snapshots are readable only by the server's administrator account. They are not currently deleted on a fixed schedule; the operator removes them manually.
  • Data on your devices: stays until you clear it, sign out or uninstall the app.

10. Your choices and rights

10.1 In the app

  • Export: Settings → Data & backup lets you download all your data as JSON, or as a ZIP that includes your photos and videos.
  • Correct: you can edit or delete any workout, measurement, weigh-in or note, and change your display name.
  • Erase content: "Reset everything" deletes your training data, photos and videos, and AI Coach data on the server. Your profile and passkeys stay.
  • Control access: you can remove passkeys, sign out on every device, turn off notifications, and disconnect AI assistants or revoke their tokens.

10.2 Deleting your account

There is no button to delete your account yet. To delete it, email nemathsnv@gmail.com. Include the Account ID shown in Settings → Account so we can find your profile. We may ask you to confirm the request from inside the app, to make sure it really comes from you. We will then delete your profile, passkeys, push subscriptions, training data, photos and videos, and AI connections. We will do this without undue delay and at the latest within one month. Copies in backups and logs are then removed on the schedule in section 9.

10.3 Your rights under the GDPR

You have the right to:

  • access your personal data;
  • have it corrected;
  • have it erased;
  • restrict how we process it;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that happened before.

To use any of these rights, email us. We will reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in particular in the EU country where you live, where you work, or where you think the problem happened.

11. Security

We protect your data with:

  • sign-in with passkeys, which are public-key based and cannot be phished like passwords;
  • password hashing with scrypt, where passwords are used;
  • HTTPS for all connections;
  • a session cookie that is signed and that page scripts cannot read;
  • strict browser security headers, including a Content-Security-Policy;
  • data files on the server that only the Gymly service account can read;
  • restricted administrative access to the server;
  • client-side encryption of off-site backups.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the authorities where the law requires it.

12. International transfers

Your account and training data are stored in the European Union. Some providers are based in the United States or run global networks: Cloudflare, the push services, GitHub and, if you turn them on, AI providers. Using them can transfer personal data outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, the transfer relies on the protections those providers offer, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

13. Children

Gymly is not intended for children under 16. Please do not create a profile if you are under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when Gymly changes how it handles personal data, including before the Oura integration launches. The date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes, we will post a notice on this page before they take effect. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent again.

15. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, or to use your rights, email nemathsnv@gmail.com.
